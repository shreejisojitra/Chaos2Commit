How it works

From idea to live software in six clear steps.

1

Describe your idea

Paste a business idea, upload documents, or mix Hindi / Gujarati / English. The AI understands intent.

2

AI Consultant

Get explicit requirements, missing gaps, recommendations, and clarifying questions — all editable.

3

Approve blueprint

Review product overview, modules, roles, flows, and non-functional requirements. Approve when ready.

4

Architecture & UI

See system diagrams, ER views, wireframes, and sitemap generated from your approved blueprint.

5

Generate application

A real Node app with auth, CRUD, and modules is produced and verified to run — not a mockup.

6

Modify & version

Add features like attendance incrementally. Every change creates a version you can view, compare, or restore.
